Cookie Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

We use cookies to help improve your experience of Pasta Recipes Healthy. This cookie policy is part of Pasta Recipes Healthy's privacy policy, and covers the use of cookies between your device and our site.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small piece of data that a website stores on your device when you visit, typically containing information about the website itself, a unique identifier that allows the site to recognize your web browser when you return, additional data that serves the purpose of the cookie, and the lifespan of the cookie itself.

Why we use cookies

We use cookies to enable certain functions (e.g. site performance), to provide analytics, or to store your preferences (e.g. theme). We also use cookies for advertising and marketing purposes through services like Google AdSense.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Crucial for your experience of a website, enabling core features like user logins, account management, shopping carts and payment processing.
  • Performance cookies: Used in the tracking of how you use a website during your visit, without collecting personal information about you.
  • Targeting/Advertising cookies: Used in determining what promotional content is more relevant and appropriate to you and your interests.
